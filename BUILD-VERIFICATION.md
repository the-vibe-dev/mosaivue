## Hardware evidence and remaining qualification

One Raspberry Pi 3B+ running 32-bit Bullseye is running Mosaivue with seven live camera tiles through OMXPlayer at 1920×1080. After restarting into the final runtime, the rotating tile completed 21 observed cuts with zero recovery restarts; sampled cuts were preconnected and took 250–325 ms, with no rotation errors. The migrated key, config, and database have expected owner and mode checks; the audit passes with a LAN HTTP advisory. The previous services and the previous Mosaivue release remain installed, and rollback was exercised during migration. Physical-display cut continuity awaits viewer confirmation.

On 2026-10-18, a ROCK Pi 4B+ running Debian 12 arm64 and kernel 6.1.115-8-rk2501 was installed with the GStreamer backend and `kmssink`; GDM3 was disabled to release HDMI. Rockchip `mppvideodec` and 1280×720/1920×1080 HDMI modes were detected. A real 1920×1080 camera stream advanced from 232 to 462 decoded frames with zero restarts. A local four-source H.264 RTSP smoke test at 640×360 per source and 1280×720 KMS output kept all sources LIVE for 15 seconds with zero restarts; a source replacement also completed while the others advanced. The repeatable check is `scripts/rockpi-smoke.py`. The startup fix links initial sources before setting KMS to PLAYING.

The first imported multi-camera attempt was inconclusive because one source produced zero frames in an independent low-latency GStreamer probe. The later stable-latency result is recorded below. Long-run camera capacity, boot recovery, and rotation timing remain to be tested. Physical DRM/KMS output on Pi 4 and Pi 5 remains unqualified. Run the [hardware test plan](docs/hardware-test-plan.md) before making broader device-specific capacity claims.

On 2026-10-19 UTC, the ROCK Pi 4B+ completed a second four-source KMS smoke test with two prepared source switches and zero restarts. The real 1920×1080 imported seven-tile wall then completed eight observed switches between Garage Inside and SE with all seven streams LIVE and zero renderer restarts. The site viewer confirmed that both rotating cameras appeared on HDMI. Both rotating camera profiles were changed from `low` to `stable` after independent GStreamer probes confirmed that their video needed a larger jitter buffer. The GStreamer input selector now keeps both rotation sources running, switching only when the next source has a recent decoded frame. A database backup before the profile change is under the device's rollback directory. A later active-source error exposed a blocking RTSP teardown in the GStreamer bus callback; a follow-up routes that failure to the prepared alternate and keeps the video loop responsive. The hotfixed wall then completed 11 sampled switches with all seven streams LIVE and zero recovery or service restarts. A second observed camera failure and long-run recovery still need testing.

The next clean install exposed a separate prepared-source error: its branch remained unready and the rotation stopped while the other six fixed tiles continued. Subsequent source errors showed that fixed-stream recovery could also block the shared loop. The follow-up resets failed prepared and fixed RTSP sources with GStreamer's worker-thread `call_async`. On the ROCK Pi, a manual restart of fixed slot `import-slot-0` returned from RECONNECTING to LIVE with its restart count increasing to 1; all seven tiles remained LIVE, and the rotation advanced from switch 8 to 9. A separate two-source local RTSP recovery test exercised an intentionally failed prepared branch and a fixed-source reset with `fakesink`; both finished LIVE, with two prepared switches, one fixed restart, and no renderer stall. The real seven-tile wall continued through 14 rotation switches with seven LIVE streams and no service restarts.

After a clean install of commit `7f2594b`, both active and prepared rotation branches reported `Internal data stream error` at 00:57:41 UTC. The renderer RPC stayed responsive and six fixed tiles kept advancing, but the rotation slot froze at switch 6 while still reporting LIVE. The renderer was restarted manually to restore the slot. The follow-up marks a stale rotation source STALLED and requests a systemd restart if both sources remain unavailable after a recovery grace period.

A later dual-feed failure exercised the follow-up: the renderer opened a fresh prepared source, logged that both feeds remained unavailable, and systemd restarted it. The seven-tile wall returned LIVE. A separate standby-only failure left the active camera advancing while two fresh standby branches failed to decode. A bounded, five-minute-cooldown restart was added for that case; its live behavior remains to be observed.

After the cooldown applied to both failure paths, a hotfixed renderer completed eight sampled alternating switches with all seven slots LIVE and no service restarts. `scripts/rockpi-smoke.py --streams 2 --seconds 15 --sink fakesink --recovery` passed on the same device after the source-identity changes: two prepared switches, one fixed-source reset, and both local streams LIVE. The cooldown itself has a unit test; a second live gateway outage is needed to establish its long-run effect.

Further tracing separated two errors that had shared the same dashboard symptom. An inactive `videotestsrc` placeholder reported `not-negotiated`; treating it as a camera error could cause an unnecessary rotation failover, so placeholder messages are now excluded from camera recovery. The UniFi `rtspsrc` also reported `not-negotiated` at times. SE independently decoded 239 frames in eight seconds with FFprobe and 297 frames in ten seconds with a separate GStreamer player on the ROCK Pi, so the source itself was reachable during those probes. Its two audio and one video RTSP tracks were confirmed; a video-only selection experiment did not improve the full wall and was reverted. The full seven-stream MPP/compositor configuration remains subject to intermittent RTSP negotiation errors; long-run rotation continuity is not qualified by the short smoke results.
